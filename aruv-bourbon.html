Crafted for quiet interiors

The Aruv Bourbon kennel is assembled without a single screw — each panel clicks into place through precision-cut slot-lock joints milled from first-quality 9 mm Baltic Birch Plywood. The result is a structure of unexpected rigidity and warmth that feels at home beside Scandinavian furniture as much as rustic farmhouse interiors.

Every surface is treated with Rubio Monocoat® Plant Oil — a certified zero-VOC, zero-formaldehyde finish that is completely safe for pets and children. The coating enhances the natural birch grain while protecting it from daily wear.

Cushion and canopy fabrics in Indigo Blue and White are machine-washable and removable. Each kennel ships flat-pack with a detailed installation guide; no tools are required. Individual parts can be ordered separately if needed.

  • Installs without screws, bolts or glue — slot-lock joinery only
  • Rubio Monocoat® certified: health & toy safe, zero VOC

Material

First-quality 9 mm Baltic Birch Plywood, selected for its uniform core, consistent density, and natural warmth. Each sheet is CNC-milled for precise joinery tolerance — panels align with hand-pressure only, no mallet required.

The birch grain is a living pattern. Every kennel carries a subtly different surface character — no two pieces are identical.

Coating & Safety

All wood surfaces are finished with Rubio Monocoat® Pure Plant Oil — an industry-certified, single-coat wood finish that is:

  • Zero VOC (volatile organic compounds)
  • Zero formaldehyde
  • Health & toy safety certified
  • Food contact safe
  • Biodegradable

Care Instructions

  • Wipe wood surfaces with a slightly damp cloth
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight
  • Recommended for indoor use only
  • Remove cushion and machine-wash at 30°C
  • Do not bleach or tumble dry cushion fabric
  • Reassemble panels by hand — no tools needed
  • Contact us for support at any stage
