Design Story & Specs
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

No Screws, No Hardware.
Pure Architectural Woodwork.

Moving away from flimsy metal fasteners, rusting screws, and synthetic adhesives, ARUV BOURBON was engineered around an ancient Japanese joinery philosophy adapted for modern living: precision Slot-Lock geometry.

Each component is sculpted from premium Baltic birch using ultra-precise laser calibration. The pieces self-align and lock securely by hand in under 3 minutes with zero tools, forming an unshakeable, sculptural haven.

✦

Solid Baltic Birch

Sourced responsibly from certified renewable forests. Its multi-ply dense core prevents warping, resists claw marks, and imbues the interior with the subtle, comforting aroma of natural wood.

◈

Certified Zero-VOC

Formulated specifically for the sensitive respiratory systems and keen senses of animals. Finished exclusively with solvent-free plant oils and carnauba wax, fully compliant with EN 71-3 toy safety standards.

◉

Acoustic Cocoon

The pyramidal roof geometry naturally diffuses ambient household reverberation, creating a calm acoustic refuge that instills security during storms, gatherings, or restless evenings.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Does assembly require any screws or tools? +
None at all. The proprietary "Slot-Lock" system allows every wooden tier to slide and lock firmly into place by hand under its own structural tension. Setup takes approximately 3 minutes out of the flat-pack box.
How do I clean and maintain the wood and cushion? +
The included orthopedic cushion features a concealed zipper; its linen cover can be machine-washed on a gentle 30°C cycle. For the birch wood, simply wipe down with a soft, slightly damp cloth.
Are the wood oils and protective sealants safe for pets? +
Completely safe. We treat the timber exclusively with food-safe linseed oil and carnauba wax compounds bearing Zero-VOC ratings and certified under EN 71-3 standards. Even if licked or gnawed, it is harmless.
Can I commission bespoke dimensions or private branding? +
Yes. Our artisanal studio frequently collaborates with interior designers, boutique hospitality venues, and private collectors for custom sizing, laser engravings, and tailored wood tones. Reach out via the inquiry button to discuss your project.
