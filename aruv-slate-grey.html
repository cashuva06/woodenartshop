Design Story & Specs
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Contemporary Patina.
Artisanal Slate Grey & Baltic Birch.

Designed for modern interior spaces where refined color palettes converge with organic textures, ARUV SLATE GREY introduces a deep, weathered mineral tone to our architectural pet shelter collection.

Utilizing our signature Slot-Lock interlocking joinery, every precision-cut component connects through structural friction and balance without a single screw, nail, or mechanical fastener. Assembling cleanly by hand in under 3 minutes, it creates a grounded, restorative retreat for your companion while complementing sophisticated contemporary living spaces.

✦

Artisanal Slate Patina

Hand-applied eco-friendly mineral grey finish highlighting the natural grain and growth rings of Nordic birch without synthetic chemical lacquers or volatile solvent smells.

◈

Certified Zero-VOC

Protected exclusively with organic plant-based oils and natural waxes meeting stringent EN 71-3 safety directives. Completely odorless, lick-safe, and non-toxic for four-legged companions.

◉

Tool-Free Slot Joinery

Precision laser-calibrated interlocking slots deliver remarkable structural rigidity. Disassembles completely flat for effortless transport, vacuum cleaning, or seasonal storage.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Does assembly require any screws or tools? +
None at all. The proprietary "Slot-Lock" system allows every wooden tier to slide and lock firmly into place by hand under its own structural tension. Setup takes approximately 3 minutes out of the flat-pack box.
How is the Slate Grey finish achieved and is it pet-safe? +
The slate grey tone is achieved through natural plant and mineral pigments infused into organic plant oils. It contains zero toxic solvents, zero VOCs, and is fully certified under EN 71-3 toy safety standards.
How do I clean and maintain the wood and cushion? +
The included orthopedic cushion features a concealed zipper; its linen cover can be machine-washed on a gentle 30°C cycle. For the wood frame, simply wipe down with a soft, slightly damp cloth.
Can I commission bespoke dimensions or private branding? +
Yes. Our artisanal studio frequently collaborates with interior designers, boutique hospitality venues, and private collectors for custom sizing, laser engravings, and tailored finishes. Reach out via the inquiry button to discuss your project.
