Design Story & Specs
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Organic Purity.
Air, Light & Architectural Timber.

Conceived as an airy pavilion that celebrates the natural, unblemished radiance of Baltic birch, ARUV NATURAL distills pet furniture down to its purest architectural essence.

Utilizing our signature Slot-Lock interlocking joinery, every wooden beam slides securely into place with organic friction and tension. With zero hardware or metallic parts, the pavilion assembles effortlessly by hand in under 3 minutes, creating a well-ventilated, sunlit sanctuary for calm and restorative rest.

✦

Solid Baltic Birch

Harvested sustainably from certified renewable Nordic woodlands. Multi-layer cross-grain construction ensures exceptional rigidity, resisting seasonal expansion, warping, and everyday wear.

◈

Certified Zero-VOC

Protected exclusively with food-safe linseed oils and natural waxes, entirely free from volatile organic compounds, solvents, and synthetic chemicals. Certified under EN 71-3 safety directives.

◉

Open-Air Ventilation

The open pavilion architecture ensures unhindered airflow and daylight penetration, keeping the sanctuary pleasantly cool and airy during warm days while remaining draft-free.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Does assembly require any screws or tools? +
None at all. The proprietary "Slot-Lock" system allows every wooden tier to slide and lock firmly into place by hand under its own structural tension. Setup takes approximately 3 minutes out of the flat-pack box.
How do I clean and maintain the wood and cushion? +
The included orthopedic cushion features a concealed zipper; its linen cover can be machine-washed on a gentle 30°C cycle. For the birch wood, simply wipe down with a soft, slightly damp cloth.
Are the wood oils and protective sealants safe for pets? +
Completely safe. We treat the timber exclusively with food-safe linseed oil and carnauba wax compounds bearing Zero-VOC ratings and certified under EN 71-3 standards. Even if licked or gnawed, it is harmless.
Can I commission bespoke dimensions or private branding? +
Yes. Our artisanal studio frequently collaborates with interior designers, boutique hospitality venues, and private collectors for custom sizing, laser engravings, and tailored wood tones. Reach out via the inquiry button to discuss your project.
