Design Story & Specs
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Origami Geometry.
Mythical Grace & Layered Shadows.

Inspired by the delicate art of folded paper and mythical lore, ORIGAMI OWL bridges ancient folklore with sleek contemporary architectural sculpture. Each facet of the equine profile, stylized spiral horn, and flowing mane is translated into multi-tiered precision laser-cut MDF contours that rise gracefully outward from the wall.

As ambient daylight shifts across living or creative studio spaces, the geometric crease lines generate captivating contrasts between warm, tactile wood fiber surfaces and deep shadow valleys. Designed for zero wall damage, ORIGAMI OWL mounts cleanly without drilling, anchors, or hammers—securing in seconds via our pre-applied industrial mounting tape.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

How is the artwork mounted without wall drilling or screws? +
The artwork is engineered specifically for damage-free installation. It includes heavy-duty, surface-safe double-sided industrial mounting tape on the back. No drills, hammers, or wall holes are required—simply peel the protective film and press firmly against your clean interior wall for 30 seconds.
What materials are used and what is the layered construction? +
ORIGAMI OWL is crafted from high-density architectural MDF (Medium-Density Wood Fiberboard). Multiple precision laser-cut tiers are layered to create a tactile 3D relief, backed by a contrasting dark base layer that produces vivid depth and dynamic shadow lines under interior lighting.
How should the artwork be cleaned and maintained? +
Because it is designed for indoor living spaces, routine care simply requires a light dusting with a dry microfiber cloth or a soft bristle brush between the geometric contours. Avoid wet chemicals or soaking in water.
Can I request custom scale dimensions or wholesale boutique quantities? +
Yes. WoodenArtShop collaborates directly with interior architects, gift galleries, and concept stockists worldwide. We provide volume wholesale pricing and custom dimension scaling upon request. Click "Request Studio Quote" to submit your specifications.
