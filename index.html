Studio Heritage

Crafted with quiet patience, never rushed.

At WoodenArtShop, we believe modern spaces hunger for the grounded authenticity of real wood. We reject plastic mass production in favor of slow, deliberate craftsmanship. Every log is carefully selected for its natural character, knots, and striking grain flows.

🪵

Sustainably Rescued Timber

We work exclusively with certified responsible forestry logs, storm-fallen trees, and historical reclaimed timber.

Traditional Hand Joinery

Organic Non-Toxic Finishes
